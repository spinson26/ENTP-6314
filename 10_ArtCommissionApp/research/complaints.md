# Complaint Themes & What Humans Are Paid to Compile Art Briefs

**Prepared:** September 22, 2026
**Scope:** Complaints from the last 18 months (since March 22, 2025) about apps offering your feature set — photograph the space, choose size/color/theme/texture, upload inspiration, vetted artists, AI-generated brief with a human making the final work — plus what companies pay humans to do that briefing work.

> **Access note.** Reddit is blocked (the biggest gap in this dossier — commission culture lives there). G2 is blocked. Capterra covers gallery-management software sold to galleries, not these tools. Evidence is **390 critical (≤3★) App Store reviews since March 22, 2025, across 13 apps**. Complaints are paraphrased with app, rating, date and handle; originals are in [appstore-reviews-18mo.json](appstore-reviews-18mo.json).

---

## No app has all these features — so this uses four analog groups

I could not find a single product combining room capture, size/palette/theme selection, inspiration upload, credential-vetted artists and AI-brief-to-human-painting. **The combined product does not exist.** So the complaint evidence is grouped by the feature each analog actually ships:

| Feature in your spec | Analog apps used | Critical reviews in window |
|---|---|---|
| **Photograph the space + AI transforms it** | Renovate AI, Home AI, Paint AI | **174** |
| **See art at the right size on your wall** | Smartist, Artrooms, ArtStage | **63** |
| **Commission a vetted human + escrow** | Fiverr | **70** |
| **Artist community / AI-generated imagery / art as investment** | ArtStation, Artfol, Artist.ai, Peggy, Singulart, Masterworks | **83** |

### Theme 1 — AI output quality — 148 (38%) — **PRODUCT problem**

The largest theme, and the one aimed squarely at your core feature.

1. **Home AI, 2★, Sept 18 2026 (*Alder89*)** — the entire review is that **Gemini does the job far better than the dedicated app**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6464476667?see-all=reviews))
2. **Renovate AI, 1★, Jul 30 2026 (*'W*)** — the app **degrades the image every time you make an edit**; envisioning a remodel means watching quality fall with each change. Echoed Aug 3 2026 (*Dotdotdotkkk*): after an update everything comes out blurry with no way to fix it. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1661709494?see-all=reviews))
3. **Home AI, 1★, Sept 18 2026 (*Not A Big App Gamer*)** — most generated images are bad, and in some cases **the output doesn't even look like the same room**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6464476667?see-all=reviews))

> **Also:** Renovate AI, 2★, Sept 4 2026 (*Grl4tidr*) — **one photo can't capture a whole space**, so suggestions use areas that aren't usable. Artist.ai, 1★, Sept 2 2026 (*Jxrdnonly*) — bad at generating from **reference images**, which is exactly your "upload inspiration" feature. Artist.ai, 3★, Jul 8 2026 (*Mercs_Fubah*) — anatomy errors: animals with two heads, a bird with three wings.

**Why this matters most:** your product depends on a buyer looking at an AI-rendered impression of art in their room and trusting it enough to commission a real painting. **The apps doing the visual half today are being told their output looks fake, degrades on edit, and is beaten by a free general-purpose assistant.**

### Theme 4 — Trust, vetting and scams — 56 (14%) — **PRODUCT problem**

This theme is the direct test of your credential-vetting feature.

1. **Fiverr, 1★, Sept 14 2026 (*Ryan.Forist*)** — **$600 lost because they trusted the platform to vet its sellers**, even one with high ratings; says the platform did nothing. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id346080608?see-all=reviews))
2. **Fiverr, 1★, Sept 21 2026 (*Pris9674*)** — scammed by a seller, provided proof, got ticket runaround, going to the bank instead. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id346080608?see-all=reviews))
3. **Peggy, 2★ (buyer)** — won a bid at the artist's $150 asking price, then the artist raised the ask to $750 and the bid was voided; the platform said that was allowed. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1584224807?see-all=reviews))

> **The lesson for your vetting feature:** Fiverr already vets, rates and escrows — and 14% of its recent complaints are still about being defrauded. **Buyers do not experience "vetted" as a promise about taste or credentials; they experience it as a promise that they won't lose their money.** A degree filter does not address that, and if you advertise vetting you will own the failures.

---

### Theme 5 — Artist discovery and reach — 30 (8%) — **PRODUCT problem**

Artist-side complaints about being invisible: ArtStation reviewers report broken notifications and feeds; Peggy artists report listings stuck in review for six days with no response, and minimal traction; Artfol users report crashes that block posting entirely.

---

### Theme 6 — Scale and dimension accuracy — 24 (6%) — **PRODUCT problem**

Small in count, disqualifying in effect. Reviewers of the wall-preview apps report the **scale is simply wrong** — art shown at the wrong size against a real wall despite calibration (Section 1, iArtView). **If the buyer commissions a 48×36 canvas because your preview lied about how it reads on the wall, you own a custom, non-returnable, four-figure mistake.**

## What companies pay humans to compile art briefs

This work exists as a paid profession, in three shapes.

### Job A — Percentage of the artwork (the art advisor / consultant model)

| Model | Fee | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Art consulting firms | **5%–20% of purchase price**, sliding down as value rises | [TurningArt](https://blog.turningart.com/art-consultants) |
| Typical advisory commission | **~10%** on works up to about $5M; as low as 5% above $1M; up to **20% under $100,000** | [Mason Lane Art Advisory](https://www.masonlaneart.com/blog/how-art-advisors-make-money) |
| Hourly alternative | **$150–$500+/hour**, though hourly is falling out of favor | [Art Services Network](https://www.artservicesnetwork.com/guide/art-advisor-fees-explained-what-collectors-should-understand/) |

**Worked example:** on a **$5,000 commissioned painting**, a 10–20% advisory fee is **$500–$1,000** for exactly the work your app proposes to automate — understanding the space, the client's taste, and translating it into a brief an artist can execute.

### Job B — Salaried brief-compilers

| Role | Pay | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Art buyer** (agency/corporate — sources artwork, works with artists, negotiates) | **$74,277 avg**; $59,044–$94,703 typical; up to $116,992 | [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/art-buyer-salary-SRCH_KO0,9.htm) |
| **Corporate art buyer** | **$72,133 avg**; $57,500–$89,000 | [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Corporate-Art-Buyer) |
| **Art producer** (commissions and manages artwork production) | **$81,354 avg**; $61,639–$108,292 | [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/art-producer-salary-SRCH_KO0,12.htm) |
| **Creative producer** | **$92,269–$98,215 avg**; up to $158,287 | [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/creative-producer-salary-SRCH_KO0,17.htm); [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Creative-Producer-Salary) |
| **Gallery director** | **$72,850–$82,904 avg**; up to $142,034 | [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/gallery-director-salary-SRCH_KO0,16.htm) |
| **Interior designer** (BLS, the largest group doing this work) | **median $63,490** (May 2024); **87,100 jobs** | [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-Design/interior-Designers.htm) |

### What this says about the product

**The brief-compiling job is real, professional, and priced two ways at once:**

- **As labor:** $63,000–$98,000 a year — roughly **$30–$47 an hour** for the person who turns a space and a taste into an artwork specification.
- **As a transaction fee:** **5–20% of the artwork**, which is *lower* than the gallery's 30–50% your premise is attacking. **Art advisors already undercut galleries.** You are not entering an unpriced market; you're entering one where the incumbent middleman charges 10% and the premium middleman charges 50%.

**The strategic read:** at 10–20% of a $5,000 commission, your app's natural price is **$500–$1,000 per successful commission**, not a monthly subscription. That is a transaction-fee business — which also means you only get paid when a commission actually completes, and every complaint in Theme 4 above is about what happens when it doesn't.
