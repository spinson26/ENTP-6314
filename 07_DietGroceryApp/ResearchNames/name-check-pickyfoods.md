# Name Check: PickyFoods

**Date run:** 2026-10-02 · **Why:** the landing page (`LandingPage/index.html`) now uses the name PickyFoods, which was not one of the 75 names in the first screen.

## Verdict

**PickyFoods is not taken as an exact name, but it sits in a crowded field and would be hard to protect.** No app or live trademark uses the exact name, and pickyfoods.app is open. But pickyfoods.com has been registered since 2016. At least 20 US apps already use "Picky," many of them for food, and two pending trademark applications claim "PICKY" for food and diet apps. "Picky" also signals picky eaters and kids, which doesn't fit a weight-loss and GLP-1 audience.

**Risk rating: medium to high.** That's fine for a class demo page. For a launch, it ranks below SatietyCart and HushPlate.

## Food and diet apps already using "Picky"

On the US App Store, a search for "picky" turned up 22 apps with Picky in the title. These are the ones in the same space ([iTunes Search API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=picky&entity=software&country=us)):

- [Eat Picky](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/eat-picky/id6757623234) (Health & Fitness, released 2026-03-26) is a food-allergy app with a barcode scanner and AI scanning.
- [Picky - What's for dinner?](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picky/id1532620638) lets users swipe through recipes and match on what to eat. It has been on the store since 2021.
- [Picky - Grocery Shopping List](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picky-grocery-shopping-list/id1098716548) is a grocery list app that has been on the store since 2017.
- [Picky - Safe Eating](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picky-safe-eating/id6801220098) reads restaurant menus for people with allergies. It was released in September 2026.
- [Picky Plates](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picky-plates/id6757972982) and [EatPal: Picky Eater Meal Plans](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/eatpal-picky-eater-meal-plans/id6762243166) are meal planners with grocery lists for families with picky children.
- [PickyEatrs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pickyeatrs/id6759879599), [A Little Picky?](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/a-little-picky/id6753876099) and [PickyPlaces](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pickyplaces/id6763200538) are also in Food & Drink.

On Google Play, a search for "picky food" turned up the package IDs `recipes.picky.app`, `com.pickycode.pickyeats` and `com.pickychefs.app` ([Play Store search](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=picky%20food&c=apps&hl=en_US&gl=US)).

## What this means for the name

- **Search and app-store discovery:** someone searching "Picky" for this app would find 20 or more others first.
- **Trademark:** an application for PICKYFOODS in class 9 would probably be compared with the PICKY? and PICKY CHEFS applications that were filed earlier. "Foods" adds nothing an examiner can rely on to tell them apart.
- **Positioning:** across the App Store, "picky" means picky eaters, kids and allergies. The product's buyers are adults losing weight, many on GLP-1s. The name points them at the wrong use.
- **Domain:** the .com has been held for 10 years and is renewed through 2031. Buying it would mean tracking down the owner and paying an unknown price. Launching on pickyfoods.app or getpickyfoods.com would work.

## What could not be verified

- **Who owns pickyfoods.com, and whether it's for sale:** the registrant is hidden behind GoDaddy privacy.
- **The outcome of the PICKY? and PICKY CHEFS applications:** both are pending, and either could still be abandoned or refused.
- **State trademarks, common-law use, foreign marks, non-US app stores and social handles:** not checked. The same limits as the first screen apply.
- **Google Play titles:** the exact-name check found nothing. The broader "picky food" Play search was read only from package IDs, not full listings.
