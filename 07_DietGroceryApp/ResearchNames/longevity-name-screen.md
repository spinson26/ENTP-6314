# Longevity Name Screening Research

**Date run:** 2026-10-14 · **Candidates screened:** 70 · **Fuzzy trademark pass:** 19 finalists · **Finalists web-searched:** 14

## How the names were generated

Candidates came from six longevity and lifestyle angles:

- **Time and age:** long, ever, century, hundred, decades, years, ripe, ageless.
- **Healthspan words:** span, healthspan, lifespan.
- **Longevity cultures:** Okinawa, *hara hachi bu* (eat until 80% full), the Mediterranean and olives, sage.
- **Nature that lives long:** old-growth forest, grove, perennial, root, tortoise.
- **Cell biology:** telomeres, mitochondria, polyphenols, fiber.
- **The AI agent's job:** cart, pantry, larder, kitchen, table, plate.

## How "not already taken" was tested

This used the same automated check as the first screen (`_tools_name-check.js`, raw output in `longevity-name-screen-results.json`):

- **Apple App Store (US):** [iTunes Search API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=hundredtable&entity=software&country=us).
- **Google Play (US):** [Play Store search](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=hundredtable&c=apps&hl=en_US&gl=US), checking result titles and package IDs.
- **USPTO:** [Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/), live marks only. First an exact-name pass, then a fuzzy pass on finalists (edit distance 1, plus the name as a phrase or word stem), keeping marks in classes 5, 9, 29–31, 35, 39 and 42–44. Results are in `longevity-near-marks-results.json`.
- **Domains:** [Verisign .com RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/hundredtable.com) and [Google Registry .app RDAP](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/hundredtable.app). A 404 means unregistered.
- **Crowded-word check:** the USPTO was searched for live marks on BLUE ZONES (56), HEALTHSPAN (42) and IKIGAI (27).

## Web search results for finalists

- **Telomeal:** no company or product. All results were telomere science ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12756119/)).
- **Hundred Table:** no exact match. Nearby names were One Hundred Tables, a 2013 restaurant directory ([Atlanta Magazine](https://www.atlantamagazine.com/dining-news/one-hundred-tables-launches-as-an-alternative-to-opentable-and-yelp/)), and the Hundred Burgers chain in Spain ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_Burgers)).
- **Olive Years:** no company. Results were about olive trees and olive magazine ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_Tree_of_Mouch%C3%A3o)).
- **Longlarder:** no company. Results were dictionary entries for "larder" plus Le Larder, a UK retailer with a different name.
- **Eightyfull:** no company. Results explained *hara hachi bu* ([Big Think](https://bigthink.com/personal-growth/the-japanese-practice-that-will-change-your-eating-habits/), [Unseen Japan](https://unseen-japan.com/hara-hachi-bunme/)).
- **Spanplate:** results were all German "Spanplatte" (particleboard) pages ([OBI](https://www.obi.de/p/5553151/dekorspanplatte-280-cm-x-207-cm-x-1-6-cm-eiche-astig-zuschnitt)). Ruled out on meaning.
- **Ripe Years:** no company. Results were RIPE NCC, the internet registry ([RIPE forum](https://forum.ripe.net/t/35-years-of-ripe/953)).
- **Lifelong Larder, Century Pantry:** no company. The only near hit was a cabinet-organizer line from Century Components.
- **Oldgrowth Kitchen:** no company. Results defined "old growth" forest.
- **Okiplate, Seasonspan, Steadfork:** no results at all.

## What could not be verified

- **State trademarks, unregistered use, and foreign marks (EUIPO, WIPO):** not checked.
- **Non-US app stores:** not checked.
- **Social handles on Instagram and TikTok:** not checked.
- **Domains:** "unregistered" is a snapshot from 2026-10-14. Register the chosen name promptly.
- **Owners of registered .com domains** (longplate.com, evertable.com and others) and whether they're for sale: not looked up.
- **Health-claim exposure:** whether a given name counts as an implied health claim is a legal judgment. The FTC note in the name list is general guidance, not advice for this app.
