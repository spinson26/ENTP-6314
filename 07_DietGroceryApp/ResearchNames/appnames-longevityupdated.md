# App Names, Updated: Longevity and Lifestyle Eater App

**Checked:** 2026-10-14 · **Project:** 07_DietGroceryApp · **Earlier list:** `appnames.md` (2026-09-29, GLP-1 and diet positioning)

**The repositioned product:** an app for people who eat to live longer and better, not only to lose weight. It pulls together their longevity nutrition goals (protein, fiber, plants, polyphenols, healthy fats, eating to about 80% full), recipes and lifestyle preferences. An AI agent then orders the groceries and has them delivered. GLP-1 users can still be one audience inside it.

**Why the old names don't carry over:** SatietyCart and HushPlate (the live landing-page name) are built around GLP-1 language like "satiety" and "food noise." A longevity eater thinks in terms of years, healthspan, the Mediterranean and Okinawan ways of eating, and a well-stocked kitchen. HushPlate could stay as the name of a GLP-1 product line, but it would undersell a longevity app.

**How "not already taken" was checked:** this was the same screen as the first list, run on 70 new candidates. Each name was checked on the US App Store, US Google Play, live USPTO trademarks (exact, then a fuzzy look-alike pass on 19 finalists), and .com, .app and get[name].com registration. The top finalists were also web-searched. Method, sources and the full table are in `AppNames_Research-LONGEVITYUPDATED.docx`.

**This is a first screen, not a legal clearance.** Have a trademark attorney run a full search before you commit.

---

## Words to stay away from

Several obvious longevity words are already owned or crowded. A name built on them would be hard to register and could draw a challenge.

- **Blue Zones:** 56 live US trademarks, most owned by Blue Zones, LLC, including software (class 42) and health services (class 44).
- **Healthspan:** 42 live marks, including HEALTHSPAN in software classes 9, 35 and 42. That's why Healthspan Cart and Healthspan Kitchen were ruled out even though their domains are open.
- **Ikigai:** 27 live marks.
- **Telomere and lifespan claims:** a name that promises a biological result (longer telomeres, more years) invites questions about whether the app can back that up. The FTC requires health claims to have competent and reliable scientific evidence behind them ([FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance)). Names that suggest a way of life are safer than names that suggest a medical outcome.

## Checked and ruled out

- **Healthspan Cart, Healthspan Kitchen:** the domains are open, but the word HEALTHSPAN is crowded with 42 live marks, some in software classes.
- **Spanplate:** "Spanplatte" is German for particleboard, and that's all web search returns.
- **Slowage:** "Slow Age – Food Aging Scanner" already exists on both stores.
- **Vitaspan:** live VITASPAN mark (class 5), plus a "VitaSpan" Medicare app.
- **Aevum:** three App Store apps, six Play apps and five live marks.
- **Perennia, Spanwise, Primeplate, Tenthdecade, Tortoise Table, Oldgrowth:** an app with that name already exists on at least one store.
- **Longevora, Aeviva, Evergrain, Everplate, SpanCart, Vivacart, Fiberfirst:** a live USPTO mark already exists under the exact name.

## Recommendation

**Lead with Hundred Table. Keep Olive Years as the backup.** Hundred Table is a lifestyle, not a diet. It points at the "live to 100" goal without making a medical promise, and it suits the shared-meal, Mediterranean way of eating. All its domains are open, and nothing in software or food uses the name.

**If you want to keep the GLP-1 users from the first version, use Eightyfull.** "Eat until 80% full" is a centuries-old longevity habit and a good description of how GLP-1 users already eat. That makes it the one name on this list that serves both audiences.

Next steps for whichever name you pick:
- Register the .com, .app and get- domains.
- File a USPTO intent-to-use application in classes 9, 35 and 44.
- Have counsel run a full clearance search.
- If the brand moves away from HushPlate, update the landing page at hushplate.vercel.app.
