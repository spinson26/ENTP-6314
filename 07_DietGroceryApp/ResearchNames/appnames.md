# App Names: Diet, Nutrition, Recipe and AI Grocery Ordering App

**Checked:** 2026-09-29 · **Project:** 07_DietGroceryApp

**The product being named:** one app that pulls together a dieter's nutrition targets, recipes and meal plan, including support for people on GLP-1 medications, and then uses an AI agent to order the groceries and have them delivered.

**How "not already taken" was checked:** each name was searched on the US Apple App Store, US Google Play, the USPTO trademark database (live marks only), and the .com and .app domain registries, and the finalists were also run through a web search. Method, the full 75-name table and sources are in `name-screen.md` and in `AppNames_Research.docx`.

**Important:** this is a first screen. It is not a legal trademark clearance. Before spending money on a name, have a trademark attorney run a full search (state registrations, unregistered "common-law" use, and foreign marks).

## Names that were checked and ruled out (already taken)

- **Nourivo:** an existing iOS/Android app, "Nourivo: GLP-1 & Protein." That's a direct conflict.
- **Dosely:** several GLP-1/peptide tracker apps, plus a live DOSELY trademark (class 5).
- **DoseWise:** four App Store apps, including a GLP-1 dose tracker.
- **Macrova, PantryPilot, PlatePilot, PantryWise, Pantrymind, Larderly, Pantrist, Proteinly, Portionly, Steadyplate, Fillwell, Smallbite, Wellstocked, Restockr, Grocivo, MacroCart:** each already has a food, nutrition or pantry app under that name.
- **Satiate, Fedwell, Tinyplate, Satiety Kitchen, Cartfull, Tessellate, Plenara, Dietdrop, Provisioner:** each has a live USPTO mark, several of them in software or food classes.
- **CartPilot, Leanvia:** the .com is an active business under the same name.
- **Pantrova:** PANTROVE is a live mark in classes 9 and 42 (software).

## Recommendation

**Lead with SatietyCart. Keep HushPlate as the backup.** SatietyCart is the only finalist that names both the GLP-1 benefit and the ordering agent, and its .com, .app and get- domains are all open. HushPlate is the stronger brand for a GLP-1-first launch, because "food noise" is the words those users already use. Before announcing either name: register the .com and .app, file a USPTO intent-to-use application in classes 9 (app software), 35 (online grocery ordering services) and 44 (nutrition information), and have counsel run a full clearance search.
