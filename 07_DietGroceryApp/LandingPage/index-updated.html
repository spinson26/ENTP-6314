HushPlate
Concept preview

For anyone losing weight, including on GLP-1s

Losing weight means juggling a tracker, a recipe app, and a grocery app that don't talk to each other.

HushPlate pulls your nutrition targets, recipes, and meal plan into one place. Then an AI agent builds the grocery order for you.

  • One plan built around your targets

    Calories, protein, allergies, and food dislikes in one profile, with smaller, protein-first portions if you're on a GLP-1.

  • Recipes that already fit

    Every meal is picked to match your plan, so you're not checking each recipe's numbers yourself.

  • Groceries ordered for you

    An AI agent turns the week's meals into a cart at your store. You review it and approve before anything is bought.

Illustrative mockups. Meals, prices and numbers are examples.

HushPlate is a concept in development. Not medical advice; talk to your doctor about your diet and medication.
